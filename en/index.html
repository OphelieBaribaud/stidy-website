Frequently asked questions

Is Stidy free?

Yes, Stidy has a free version to track your habits and tasks. An optional "Plus" subscription unlocks extra features, with a free trial.

What is the difference between a habit and a task?

A habit repeats (daily, weekly or monthly) and builds a streak. A task is a one-off: you check it off once, like in a to-do list. Stidy brings both together in one place.

Do I need an account to use Stidy?

No. You can start right away without an account, then link your account to Apple or Google to recover your data.

Are my notes and habits private?

Your journal notes are encrypted and you choose what your friends see: you can hide a habit or a memory at any time.

Which phones is Stidy available on?

Stidy is coming soon on iOS and Android, in French and English.

Your privacy first

You can start using Stidy without creating an account. Your journal notes are encrypted, you choose what your friends see, and we never sell any data. Everything is detailed in our privacy policy.
